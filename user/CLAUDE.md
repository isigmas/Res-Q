Here is description of Res-Q project:
"In the mountains, "close" isn't close enough.

Standard GPS can be off by 20 meters in difficult terrain and rescuers never know what landscape they’ll face until they’re already in it. In fog or at night, those 20 meters are the difference between life and death. Our app is a "point-to-target" navigation tool for rescuers that turns guesswork into certainty. We guide rescuers directly to the objective with meter-level precision, enriched with real-time information about the surrounding terrain and landscape for safer, faster decision-making.

Built on EU Space Technology

Res-Q gives rescue teams a complete operational picture. Users can trigger an SOS with one tap, instantly transmitting their exact position and status. Rescuers receive a live, high-precision location stream, terrain context from satellite imagery, and real-time hazard alerts such as snow cover and severe weather

Built for the Seconds That Save Lives

Res-Q is made for the moments when every second counts. A system that removes uncertainty, shortens response times, and brings the advantages of space technologies directly into the hands of mountain rescuers."

Additionally, app has more features, such as offline map downloading, weather radar that works even without the Internet (it fetches weather data regulary, so it is avaiable from memory), Sharing location to other users (e.g. family member) and route planning (navigation)
